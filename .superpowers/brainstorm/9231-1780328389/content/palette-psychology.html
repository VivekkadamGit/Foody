🧠 The Science of Food Colors

Warm reds & oranges stimulate the appetite — that's why McDonald's, KFC, Zomato, and Swiggy all use red. Cream/off-white backgrounds reduce eye strain for long reading. Pure black text on warm cream is 20% easier to read than on pure white (less glare). Cold colors like blue suppress hunger.
